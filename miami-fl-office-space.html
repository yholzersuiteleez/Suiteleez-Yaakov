Commercial Real Estate Market in Miami: What You Need to Know Miami's Top Commercial Districts

Miami's commercial landscape spans from the gleaming towers of Brickell and Downtown to the creative corridors of Wynwood and the suburban campuses of Coral Gables and Doral. Each district caters to different industries and company profiles, creating a diverse ecosystem of office environments.

Current Market Conditions and Pricing

Miami's office market remains one of the strongest in the Southeast. Vacancy rates in prime districts hover around 1014%, among the lowest in the nation. Class A space in Brickell commands $5575 per square foot annually, while emerging areas like Wynwood offer $3550 per square foot. Landlords are investing heavily in amenities to attract quality tenants. What's Driving Demand in Miami Financial services migration from the Northeast, Latin American business connections, the growing tech scene, and favorable Florida tax policies (no state income tax) all drive sustained demand. Miami's position as a gateway to Latin America adds unique international business value.
