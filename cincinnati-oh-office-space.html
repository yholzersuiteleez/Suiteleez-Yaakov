Cincinnati Office Market Overview Cincinnati, Ohio stands as one of America's most dynamic and affordable commercial real estate markets. Home to Fortune 500 powerhouses like Procter & Gamble, Kroger, Fifth Third Bancorp, and Western & Southern, Cincinnati offers corporate tenants exceptional value without sacrificing quality, location, or amenities. Cincinnati's commercial office sector encompasses approximately 145 million square feet across the metropolitan area, making it a substantial market with significant depth and diversity.
