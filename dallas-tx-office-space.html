Commercial Real Estate Market in Dallas: What You Need to Know Dallas's Top Commercial Districts

The DFW metroplex spreads across a vast area, creating distinct commercial nodes. Uptown Dallas has emerged as the premier walkable urban business district. Downtown retains the city's largest concentration of Class A towers. Legacy West in Frisco and Las Colinas in Irving serve as major suburban corporate centers.

Current Market Conditions and Pricing

Dallas's office market is expansive and generally tenant-favorable. Vacancy rates hover around 1822%, reflecting significant new construction. Uptown Class A commands $3855 per square foot annually, Downtown ranges from $3048/sq ft, and suburban centers like Legacy West sit at $3245/sq ft. Deep Ellum creative space offers $2840/sq ft. What's Driving Demand in Dallas Corporate relocations from California and the Northeast continue at a strong pace, driven by Texas's tax advantages and lower operating costs. Financial services, technology, telecommunications AT&T, professional services, and healthcare all contribute to demand. The DFW metroplex's population growth exceeds 100,000 annually.
